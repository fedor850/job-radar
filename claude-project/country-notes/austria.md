# Austria: job-search notes

Not legal advice. For decisions check with your university's international office, the AMS or ABA (workinaustria.com).
Write the equivalent file for your own country and upload it to the Claude Project as `Country_Notes.md`.

## Work authorisation (third-country students with a residence permit "Aufenthaltsbewilligung – Student")
- Up to 20 h/week: the EMPLOYER applies to the AMS for an employment permit (Beschäftigungsbewilligung). Up to 20 h the
  AMS checks formal criteria only (no labour-market test). The permit is tied to that employer, max. 1 year, extendable.
- More than 20 h/week is possible in principle, but the AMS may run a labour-market test (Ersatzkraftverfahren). That
  is slower and uncertain.
- Internships: an internship credited towards the studies, and a voluntary internship (Volontariat) of up to 3 months,
  are handled by an AMS notification (Anzeigebestätigung) filed by the employer ≥ 3 weeks before the start.
- What to tell employers: "I hold an Austrian student residence permit. For up to 20 h/week you only need a standard
  AMS employment permit (no labour-market test). I can share the details."
- EU/EEA/Swiss citizens need no permit.

## Job ads & pay
- Austrian ads must state the minimum pay under the collective agreement (KV) and whether overpayment is possible,
  usually "auf Vollzeitbasis (38.5 h)". Pro-rate it to your hours (salary × hours / 38.5).
- Vocabulary: "Vollzeit" = full-time (38.5–40 h); "Teilzeit" = part-time; "geringfügig" = marginal employment (very
  few hours); "Werkstudent / studentische:r Mitarbeiter:in" = working student; "Praktikum" = internship;
  "Pflichtpraktikum" = mandatory internship; "Traineeprogramm" = graduate programme (usually full-time).
- "(m/w/d)", "(all genders)": mandatory gender tags, ignore them.

## Application conventions
- International companies: English CV, no photo needed. Austrian companies (German ads) often expect German. With
  limited German, target roles where English is the working language or German is "a plus".
- Most applications go through the company's ATS (Workday, SmartRecruiters, SuccessFactors…): upload a PDF. Forms
  often ask for availability (hours/week), start date and salary expectation.
- Salary expectation for student roles: the KV minimum from the ad, or a range slightly above it for your hours.
- "Initiativbewerbung" = unsolicited application. It is worthwhile for target companies without a matching ad.
- University career centres (e.g. WU ZBP) have job boards, career fairs and CV checks.
